Built on tooling precision and repeatable process.

SM Plastics is a plastic components manufacturer serving industrial and consumer goods buyers who need consistent parts, run after run.

We run our own mold shop alongside our molding floor, so tooling changes, trial runs and production adjustments happen without waiting on an outside vendor. That keeps lead times predictable and puts accountability for part quality in one place.

Every job — prototype or 500,000-unit production run — moves through the same incoming-material checks, in-process monitoring and final inspection before it ships.

  • Single point of accountabilityDesign, tooling and molding under one roof, with one quality record per job.
  • Material traceabilityEvery batch of resin is logged against the certificate of analysis from the supplier.
  • Reduced wasteRegrind and closed-loop material recovery are built into standard operating procedure, not an add-on.

Quality & standards

Certifications are audited annually by accredited third-party bodies. Every part follows the same five-step check before it leaves the plant.

ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management
ISO 14001:2015 — Environmental Management
RoHS compliant materials
REACH compliant materials
ASTM material specifications on request

  1. Incoming material inspection

    Each resin batch is checked against its certificate of analysis before it reaches the floor.

  2. Mold trial & validation

    First-article samples are measured against the drawing and signed off before production starts.

  3. In-process quality checks

    Operators sample parts at fixed intervals through the run and log results against the control plan.

  4. Final inspection

    A dedicated quality pass on finished parts before they're released to packing.

  5. Packing & dispatch

    Parts are packed to the customer's specification and shipped with a quality record attached.
